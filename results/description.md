This dataset contains information on life expectancy by gender across various countries, featuring a total of 194 unique entries. The columns include the country name, life expectancy for females (le_F), and life expectancy for males (le_M). The average life expectancy for females is approximately 78.12 years, with values ranging from 66.77 to 87.97 years, while for males, the average is around 74.31 years, with a range of 63.74 to 83.22 years. The dataset provides valuable spatial data, representing life expectancy trends at the country level, making it a useful resource for demographic studies and health research.
