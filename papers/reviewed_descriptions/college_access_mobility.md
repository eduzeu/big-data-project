# college_access_mobility

This dataset contains 2,202 rows and 15 columns. Rows report preferred access and mobility estimates by college or reporting category. College attendance is assigned using the institution attended most frequently between ages 19 and 22. Parent income is mean household income when children were ages 15–19, adjusted to 2015 dollars; child income is individual labor earnings in 2014. mr_kq5_pq1 is the percentage of all students who both have bottom-quintile parents and reach the top income quintile. kq5_cond_parq1 instead conditions on having bottom-quintile parents. These are different denominators. The table also includes access shares, median incomes, upper-tail mobility and cohort trends.
