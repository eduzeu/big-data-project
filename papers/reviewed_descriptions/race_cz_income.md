# race_cz_income

This dataset contains 741 rows and 21 columns. Each row represents the commuting zone where children grew up. The table reports mean adult household and individual income ranks by race, gender and parental income for children born in 1978–1983. Estimates at parent income percentiles 25 and 75 come from a linear specification; available combinations vary by measure. The table also contains pooled and race-specific population counts from the 2000 Census. Child income metrics are grouped into 100 bins and reported at bin midpoints, with income ranks rounded to two decimals. Bins with fewer than 20 children are omitted. Commuting zones should not be described as cities.
