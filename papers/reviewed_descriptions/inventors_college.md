# inventors_college

This dataset contains 423 rows and 17 columns. Each row summarizes students' patenting outcomes for a college. The sample covers children born in 1980–1984 who attended college between ages 19 and 22, restricted to U.S. citizens as of 2013. College is the institution attended for the greatest amount of time during those four calendar years. Inventors appear on a patent application in 2001–2012 or grant in 1996–2014. Highly cited inventors are the top 5% of inventors by citations within their birth cohort. Outcomes include inventor shares, patent and citation totals, and measures by parental income quintile.
