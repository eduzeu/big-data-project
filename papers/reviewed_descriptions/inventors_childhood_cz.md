# inventors_childhood_cz

This dataset contains 741 rows and 140 columns. Each row represents a childhood commuting zone. The table reports patenting outcomes for children born in 1980–1984, pooled and grouped by gender and parental income quintile. The sample is restricted to U.S. citizens as of 2013. Childhood geography comes from the ZIP code on the parents' tax return when the child was first claimed as a dependent, typically in 1996. Parent income ranks use mean household income in 1996–2000 relative to parents of children in the same birth cohort. Cells with fewer than 250 children are omitted. The table does not impose a general restriction to children of non-inventor parents.

Source: supplied `Inventors-Codebook-Table-1.pdf, pages 1–2`.
