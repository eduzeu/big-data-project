# college_characteristics

This dataset contains 2,463 rows and 49 columns. Each row represents a super_opeid college or college group. The table contains institutional characteristics from IPEDS and the College Scorecard across several measurement years, including 2000 and 2013. Fields cover location, institutional type and tier, enrollment, tuition and fees, graduation rates, faculty salaries, SAT scores, demographics and major shares. exp_instr_[YEAR] denotes total instructional expenditures, whereas endowment_pc_2000 is endowment assets per student. asian_or_pacific_share_fall_2000 includes both Asian and Pacific Islander undergraduates. Grouped institutions generally use enrollment-weighted means, with location and tier taken from the largest college in the group.

Source: supplied `Codebook-MRC-Table-10.pdf, pages 1–3`.
