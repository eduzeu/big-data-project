# life_county_expectancy

This dataset contains 1,559 rows and 49 columns. Each row represents a county. The table reports life expectancy at age 40 for men and women by quartile of the national income distribution. Both race-adjusted and unadjusted point estimates are present: le_raceadj_* contains adjusted estimates and le_agg_* contains unadjusted estimates. The sd_le_* fields are standard errors, not cross-area standard deviations. Estimates are reported only for counties with populations larger than 25,000. County, state and commuting-zone identifiers and population fields provide geographic context; those identifiers do not make this a mixture of county, state and commuting-zone observations.
