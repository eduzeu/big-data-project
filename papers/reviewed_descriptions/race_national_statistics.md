# race_national_statistics

This dataset contains 100 rows and 69 columns. Each row represents a parent household income percentile. The table reports national outcome statistics for children born in 1978–1983, with race and gender groups stored in separate columns. Some series cover only Black and White children; race-specific series may be male, female or pooled. Counts and outcomes must be interpreted using their individual column definitions rather than as regression coefficients. Bins with fewer than ten children are suppressed; binary outcomes also suppress bins with fewer than ten children in either outcome category. Values are rounded under the disclosure protocol. Parent income percentiles describe groupings, not separate geographic areas.
